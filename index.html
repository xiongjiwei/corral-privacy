A cleaner way to manage Chrome tabs.

Corral groups related tabs, collapses inactive groups, searches tabs with Ctrl+E, and archives the ones you're done with.

Get Corral Privacy Policy

What it does

  • Groups new tabs by your rules, by site, or with AI.
  • Collapses groups you're not using so the tab bar stays readable.
  • Finds any open tab with Ctrl+E.
  • Archives tabs you're done with and restores them later.

Corral runs on your device. No analytics, ads, or tracking.
